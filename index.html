General Questions

What types of businesses qualify?

We work with active, verifiable businesses including SMEs, home‑based businesses, and registered companies.

What funding amounts are available?

Typical funding ranges from small expansion capital to structured project financing.

Do you charge upfront fees?

No upfront fees are required for preliminary review or investor introduction.

How long does the process take?

Preliminary review typically takes 24–72 hours depending on the complexity of the business.
